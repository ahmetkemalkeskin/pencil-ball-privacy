Privacy Policy

Pencil Ball · Dravex Studio · Effective date: November 4, 2026

Dravex Studio built Pencil Ball as a game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Pencil Ball does not require an account and Dravex Studio does not collect, store, or share personal information through the game.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores gameplay information such as your high score, in-game points, unlocked themes, and selected preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to preserve your progress and settings. It is not transmitted to Dravex Studio. You can remove this information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The current version may connect to Google Fonts to load visual font resources. Google may process technical request information, such as an IP address and browser or device information, according to the Google Privacy Policy. Dravex Studio does not use advertising, analytics, account, location, camera, microphone, or payment services in the current version of Pencil Ball.

Children's Privacy

Pencil Ball does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game does not provide accounts or personal-data submission features, users are not asked to provide personal information.

Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

Dravex Studio does not maintain a server-side database of Pencil Ball users. Locally stored gameplay data remains on the device until the app's data is cleared or the game is uninstalled.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
