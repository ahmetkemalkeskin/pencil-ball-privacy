Privacy Policy

Pencil Ball · Dravex Studio · Effective date: November 4, 2026

Dravex Studio built Pencil Ball as a mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Pencil Ball does not require users to create an account or submit personal information. Dravex Studio does not directly collect, store, sell, or share personal information through the current version of the game. Third-party services may process limited technical information as described below.

Data Stored on Your Device

Pencil Ball stores gameplay information such as your high score, in-game points, unlocked themes, selected theme, and game preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to preserve your progress and settings.

Locally stored gameplay information is not transmitted to Dravex Studio. You can remove this information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The current version of Pencil Ball may connect to Google Fonts to load visual font resources. When these resources are requested, Google may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser or device information, and request details according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Dravex Studio does not provide account, location, camera, microphone, or payment features in the current version of Pencil Ball.

Advertising

The current version of Pencil Ball does not display advertising or use an advertising identifier. A future version of the game may use Google AdMob to display advertising.

If advertising is enabled in a future version, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, general device information, app interactions, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information. This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, secure, and improve advertising services.

Where required by applicable law, Pencil Ball will request the user's consent before loading personalized advertising or processing information for advertising purposes. Available privacy choices will be respected.

Additional information about Google's data practices is available in the Google Privacy Policy and on Google's Business Data Responsibility page.

Analytics

The current version of Pencil Ball does not use a third-party analytics service. If an analytics service is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the game's Google Play Data safety declarations will be updated where required.

Children's Privacy

Pencil Ball does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not provide account creation or personal-data submission features.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, Dravex Studio will configure advertising and consent options according to the selected audience and applicable Google Play policies.

Data Security

Dravex Studio does not maintain a server-side database containing Pencil Ball user accounts or gameplay records. Any third-party service used by the game is responsible for protecting the information it processes according to its own privacy and security practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay information remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall Pencil Ball. Because Dravex Studio does not maintain user accounts or a server-side user database, Dravex Studio does not retain account data that requires a separate deletion request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Pencil Ball's features, third-party services, or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
